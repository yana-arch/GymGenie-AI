## Story Summary

As a fitness user during a workout session, I want the AI to automatically adjust my workout based on my current energy level and time constraints, so that I receive personalized, sustainable recommendations that match my current state.

**As a** fitness user
**I want** real-time workout adaptations
**So that** my training remains safe and effective despite changing conditions

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a user is in an active workout session
2. When they report being tired or having limited time
3. Then the AI immediately suggests workout modifications within 2 seconds
4. And modifications prioritize safety while maintaining training effectiveness
