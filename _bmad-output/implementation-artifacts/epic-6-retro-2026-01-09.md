# Epic 6 Retrospective: UI/UX Refinement & Visual Polish

**Date:** 2026-01-09
**Epic Number:** 6
**Status:** Complete

## 1. Epic Review Summary

### Successes
- **Premium Branding:** Successfully established the vibrant orange "GymGenie" identity, conveying energy and professionalism across all screens.
- **Dynamic Vibes:** Implemented a robust theme switching system that handles color scheme transitions smoothly via `ThemeCustomizer`.
- **Interactive Polish:** Micro-interactions (spring animations, scale-down on press) and tactile haptic feedback have significantly elevated the app's professional feel.
- **Focus-Oriented Dashboard:** The reorganized high-fidelity HUD in the live session successfully prioritizes active exercises while maintaining a clean, technical aesthetic.
- **Responsive Harmony:** Verified layouts across phone, tablet, and desktop, ensuring the "Atmospheric Vibe" scales beautifully.

### Challenges
- **Glassmorphism Inconsistency:** Tab panels and nested `Paper` components initially used solid backgrounds, which masked the `vibe-background` and created a 'blocky' look.
- **Service Lifecycle Management:** Discovery that some heavy background services (camera/AI loops) were not always unmounting when their UI was hidden.
- **Manual Testing Reliance:** The highly visual nature of this epic required significant manual verification, as automated tests couldn't easily validate 'premium feel'.
